Navigating Institutional Debt with Precision

Securing enterprise debt in today's banking environment requires thorough financial preparation. At Bharath Kapital, we position ourselves between ambitious business enterprises and regulated institutional lenders.

We eliminate the traditional trial-and-error approach that often damages credit profiles through multiple rejections. Instead, we diagnose financial parameters first and submit proposals only where lending appetites match.

          How Bharath Kapital Helps

          Our disciplined, four-stage financial diagnostic process minimizes rejections and accelerates institutional sanction times.

          01
          Phase 01

          Comprehensive Financial Diagnosis

          Before approaching any bank or NBFC, we conduct an exhaustive diagnostic of your financial footprint. We review your profit & loss statements, balance sheet ratios, bank statement turnover velocity, debt-service coverage (DSCR), and promoter CIBIL history.

          Outcome: Clear visibility into how bank underwriting algorithms and risk managers will view your credit risk.

          02
          Phase 02

          Debt Eligibility & Product Structuring

          Selecting the wrong debt structure leads to cash flow strain. We structure your borrowing need into the right vehicles—balancing revolving working capital limits with long-term amortizing facilities, equipment debt, or collateral-free schemes.

          Outcome: A bankable credit brief that matches your operational cash conversion cycle.

          03
          Phase 03

          Institutional Lender Mapping

          Lenders maintain fluctuating sector appetites, internal geographical caps, and collateral preferences. We match your structured credit proposal only to those institutions whose current policy criteria fit your company profile.

          Outcome: Elimination of speculative multiple filings that trigger negative bureau inquiries.

          04
          Phase 04

          Application, Sanction & Disbursement Support

          The credit journey does not end with proposal submission. We assist through documentation clarification, credit manager queries, legal vetting, title verification, valuation checks, and final loan documentation execution.

          Outcome: Smooth disbursement into your company bank account with verified sanction terms.

          Target Client Base

          Who We Serve

          Focused debt advisory for commercial operators, manufacturers, traders, and high-growth mid-market enterprises.

          Capital Heavy & Asset Backed

          Manufacturing Enterprises

          Manufacturing businesses operate with lengthy working capital cycles—from raw material purchase through work-in-progress and credit-driven client receivables.

          Typical Funding Requirements:
          • Plant & heavy machinery acquisition loans
          • Revolving Cash Credit (CC) limits based on inventory
          • Letter of Credit (LC) and Bank Guarantees (BG) for suppliers
          • Industrial factory premise acquisition debt

          Key Underwriting Parameters

          Lenders focus on machinery capacity utilization, debtor holding period, raw material price volatility buffers, and DSCR metrics over the projected term.

          Fast Turnover & Inventory

          Traders & Distributors

          Wholesale trading and distribution companies require agile credit lines to quickly capture bulk purchasing discounts and service market volume spikes.

          Typical Funding Requirements:
          • Overdraft (OD) facilities against banking conduct
          • Invoice discounting for institutional buyers
          • Warehouse inventory finance
          • Collateral-free short-term working capital

          Key Underwriting Parameters

          Underwriters examine GST return reconciliation, debtor concentration risk, inventory aging records, and average bank account credit turnover.

          Human Capital & Contracts

          Service Businesses

          IT vendors, logistics operators, facility management, and consulting firms often lack substantial physical assets, making traditional collateral loans difficult.

          Typical Funding Requirements:
          • Unsecured business loans based on EBITDA
          • Factoring and discounting of service milestone billings
          • Office infrastructure and tech equipment leasing

          Key Underwriting Parameters

          Lenders focus on corporate client quality, master service agreements (MSAs), recurring contract tenure, and historical margin stability.

          Priority Sector & Small Enterprises

          MSMEs

          Registered micro, small, and medium businesses seeking to capitalize on government support schemes, collateral exemptions, and priority sector mandates.

          Typical Funding Requirements:
          • CGTMSE covered collateral-free loans
          • Machinery term loans under subsidy frameworks
          • Micro enterprise enhancement lines

          Key Underwriting Parameters

          Udyam registration validation, minimum 3 years vintage, consistent ITR filing discipline, and absence of past restructuring or defaults.

          Early Stage Growth

          Growing Businesses & Startups

          Post-revenue enterprises with validated product-market fit seeking venture debt or working capital without substantial equity dilution.

          Typical Funding Requirements:
          • Receivables discounting against enterprise clients
          • Revenue-linked debt financing
          • Promoter asset-backed commercial credit

          Realistic Underwriting Context

          Pure pre-revenue concepts cannot obtain standard debt from Indian commercial banks. Lenders require demonstrated sales, audited financials, or personal guarantor backing.

          Mid-Market & Corporate

          Corporates & Large Entities

          Corporations requiring multi-bank syndication, consortium structuring, large Capex financing, or debt restructuring to optimize interest costs.

          Typical Funding Requirements:
          • Multiple banking consortium credit arrangements
          • Large project finance and Capex lines
          • Corporate debt takeover with margin enhancement

          Key Underwriting Parameters

          External credit rating (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE), group net worth, contingent liabilities assessment, and detailed project viability studies.
