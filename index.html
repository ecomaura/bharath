          Methodology

          How We Help

          A disciplined 4-stage advisory workflow to minimize rejections and optimize credit parameters.

          01. Financial Diagnosis

          Understand the business, financial position, funding requirement, and credit profile. We assess audited balance sheets, bank statement cash velocity, and GST reconciliations before approaching lenders.

          02. Eligibility & Structuring

          Identify the appropriate loan structure and funding requirement. Selecting whether working capital, term capex debt, or collateral-free schemes fit your genuine operating cash cycles.

          03. Lender Mapping

          Match the business profile with suitable lenders and loan products. We engage banks and NBFCs with active credit policy mandates for your specific industry sector.

          04. Application & Disbursement Support

          Assist with documentation, lender communication, follow-ups, and the journey toward disbursement. We help clarify queries and review sanction terms to final fund release.
